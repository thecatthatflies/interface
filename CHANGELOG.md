# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- `interface-guidelines`: the shared foundation. Ranked principles for people and agents, the brief, build, verify, operate workflow, evidence-tagged references, and a rendered-page audit.
- `brand-identity`: palette, logo, asset and guidelines-page generators with contrast and logo checks.
- Eleven interface-type skills: dashboard, landing page, web app, mobile app, form, AI chat, agent tool, e-commerce, content site, desktop app, and email design.
- Scripts: `audit.mjs`, `palette.mjs`, `logo.mjs`, `assets.mjs`, `book.mjs`, `form-check.mjs`, `mobile-check.mjs`, `lint-tools.mjs`, `check-product-data.mjs`, `check-email.mjs`.
- Benchmark of the foundation against no skill and against taste-skill on an on-call console.
