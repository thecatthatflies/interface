# Sources

Sources behind the [E], [S] and [P] tags in this skill. Checked November 2026. Where a source was read only through a summary, it says so.

## Empirical [E]

- Liu, C., White, R. W., and Dumais, S. (2010). Understanding web browsing behaviors through Weibull analysis of dwell time. *SIGIR '10*. 205,873 pages, over 2 billion dwell times. Read via NN/g, *How Long Do Users Stay on Web Pages?*, https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-long-do-users-stay-on-web-pages/
- Nielsen Norman Group (2018). *Scrolling and Attention*. 120 participants, over 130,000 fixations, 1920x1080. First screenful 57% of viewing time, second 17%, first three 81%. https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scrolling-and-attention/
- Morkes, J., and Nielsen, J. (1997). *Concise, Scannable, and Objective: How to Write for the Web*. 51 users; promotional-language removal +27%, concise +58%, scannable +47%, combined +124%. https://www.nngroup.com/articles/concise-scannable-and-objective-how-to-write-for-the-web/
- Deloitte and 55 (2020). *Milliseconds Make Millions*. Commissioned by Google; 37 brands, about 30 million mobile sessions; correlational. https://web.dev/case-studies/milliseconds-make-millions
- Mathur, A., et al. (2019). Dark patterns at scale. *PACM HCI* 3(CSCW). 1,818 instances on about 11,000 shopping sites. https://doi.org/10.1145/3359183
- Luguri, J., and Strahilevitz, L. (2021). Shining a light on dark patterns. *Journal of Legal Analysis* 13(1). https://doi.org/10.1093/jla/laaa006
- Nouwens, M., et al. (2020). Dark patterns after the GDPR. *CHI '20*. https://doi.org/10.1145/3313831.3376321
- Ersoy, D., et al. (2025). Investigating the impact of dark patterns on LLM-based web agents. Preprint, arXiv:2510.18113. Six agents, 41.1% average susceptibility.
- taste-skill v2.1.5 published test rounds (2026), as analysed in the family's research digest `research/02-ai-era-sources.md`: skill beat no-skill on 4 of 16 brief-rounds; model jurors only; about half a point of noise per score.

## Standards and law [S]

- Google, *Web Vitals*: LCP 2.5 s, INP 200 ms, CLS 0.1 at the 75th percentile. https://web.dev/articles/vitals
- Google, *Optimize Largest Contentful Paint*. https://web.dev/articles/optimize-lcp
- WCAG 2.2: 1.3.2 Meaningful Sequence, 1.4.3 Contrast, 1.4.10 Reflow, 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide, 2.3.1 Three Flashes, 2.3.3 Animation from Interactions, 2.4.6 Headings and Labels. https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/
- W3C, *Inaccessibility of CAPTCHA*. https://www.w3.org/TR/turingtest/
- FTC, Trade Regulation Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials, 16 CFR Part 465, effective 21 October 2024.
- FTC, Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising, 16 CFR Part 255 (revised 2023).
- FTC, Trade Regulation Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees, 16 CFR Part 464, effective 12 May 2025; limited to live-event tickets and short-term lodging. Read through law-firm summaries.
- EU Price Indication Directive 98/6/EC, Art. 6a, added by Directive (EU) 2019/2161; applies since 28 May 2022. Read through the Commission guidance summaries; national rules vary.
- GDPR Art. 7 (conditions for consent); Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).
- Open Graph protocol. https://ogp.me/
- schema.org; Google Search Central, *General structured data guidelines* (markup must represent the visible page content).

## Practitioners [P]

- Nielsen, J. (2002). *Top Ten Web Design Mistakes of 2002* (missing prices first), Alertbox. https://www.nngroup.com/alertbox/prices.html (read through summaries)
- Nielsen, J. (2006). *Show Prices for Common Scenarios*. https://www.nngroup.com/articles/show-prices-for-common-scenarios/
- Nielsen, J. (2013). *Auto-Forwarding Carousels and Accordions Annoy Users and Reduce Visibility*. https://www.nngroup.com/articles/auto-forwarding/
- NN/g (2018), page structure advice in *Scrolling and Attention* (key content and calls to action high; avoid false floors).
- GOV.UK, *Content design: planning, writing and managing content*. https://www.gov.uk/guidance/content-design

## Not verified here

- Lindgaard's "visual appeal forms quickly" and other aesthetic-first findings are summarized in `interface-guidelines/references/evidence.md`; they say nothing about trust or conversion.
- No controlled study was found that ties a specific art-direction method to conversion. The art-direction steps are judgment [J].
